02 / สรุปเพื่อเลือก paper

This paper packages the single whitening active phenylethyl resorcinol (PR) in nanoliposomes to address poor solubility and UVA instability. It provides a strong formulation-to-cell-efficacy story, including sustained release, stability, cell uptake, tyrosinase and melanin assays.

The decisive limitation is that the skin study visualizes FITC-labelled carrier, not quantitatively assayed PR. It supports skin entry but cannot establish PR flux, retention or mass balance. Interpretation is this review’s assessment, not an author claim.

Evidence scope: Full PDF extracted once with MarkItDown; Methods, Results, Tables and Figs. 5, 10 and 11 reviewed. No supplement identified. Reviewed 8 September 2026.

PR, single tyrosinase inhibitor.

Poor solubility and UVA instability.

3 Barrier

Stratum corneum limits epidermal delivery.

4 System

~130 nm lipid nanoliposome.

5 Expected

Protect PR, sustain release, improve entry.

6 Observed

Release, cell effects and deeper FITC signal; quantitative PR skin delivery missing.

08 / ตรวจ Release Study

PR-NLPs and equal PR ethanol solution were placed in 8–14 kDa dialysis bags; receptor was 400 mL PBS pH 6.8 with Tween 80, 37 ± 0.5 °C and 100 rpm. One mL samples were replaced [Section 2.4.7]. PR-NLP release was 29.90 ± 4.05% at 12 h versus 94.21 ± 2.31% for solution and approached equilibrium at 72 h [Fig.6].

Score impact: Good comparator and profile; conflicting 0.1%/1% Tween wording and dialysis limitations reduce certainty.

10 / จุดแข็งที่ใช้ในการนำเสนอ

Single active

PR keeps mechanism and formulation discussion focused.

Orthogonal evidence

Stability, release, uptake and B16F10 outcomes are connected.

Relevant skin model

Pig-skin microscopy is a sensible qualitative screen.

11 / ข้อจำกัดที่เปลี่ยนการตีความ

Surrogate mismatch

FITC behaviour is not PR behaviour; quantify PR in donor, skin layers and receptor.

Not a mass balance

Images do not provide flux, Kp, deposition or recovery.

No in-vivo whitening

Cell melanin reduction does not establish cosmetic efficacy.

Release ambiguity

Tween concentration is inconsistent between sentences.

Concerns

14 / คุณค่าสำหรับการนำเสนอ

Main critique: distinguish carrier-associated fluorescence from quantitative delivery of PR. Present Fig.6 for sustained release, Fig.10 for the biological mechanism and Fig.11 to explain exactly what imaging can and cannot establish.

Obtain next: reliable PR pKa/logP/solubility, validated human-skin PR mass balance, epidermal/dermal recovery, human melanocyte or reconstructed-pigment-skin efficacy and irritation data.

15 / คำตัดสินสุดท้าย

Recommendation: chooseable, with a delivery-evidence caveat

  • Directly fits a single-compound cosmetic whitening topic.
  • Strong physicochemical, photostability, release and cellular evidence.
  • Fig.11 is visually compelling but not quantitative PR permeation evidence.
  • Best used to propose a rigorous epidermal-retention and mass-balance study.
