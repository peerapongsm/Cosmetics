Candidate screening notes

Phenylethyl resorcinol: strong whitening candidate: sustained release, photostability, B16F10 tyrosinase/melanin results and enhanced skin entry. Its evidence is weakened by tracer-led permeation imaging rather than a full PR skin mass balance, and no in-vivo whitening result. Best proposal: a photoprotective epidermis-retentive carrier with quantitative stratum-corneum/epidermis/dermis/receptor recovery.

N-acetylglucosamine: the better novel-system discussion candidate. The older niosome work has HPLC, release, rat-skin flux and retention, but very large vesicles (about 500–4500 nm), 96% ethanol receptor medium and no direct anti-melanogenesis result. Best proposal: small deformable or ion-pair lipid carrier, tested in human/reconstructed epidermis with layer recovery, whitening efficacy, stability and irritation.
